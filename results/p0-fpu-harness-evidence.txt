 1. The guest's own status word (fnstsw / fnstenv.swd, identical in both legs) is
    0x3800 in fpuprobe/fpuprobe2 and 0x3000 in stprobe. The reference reports exactly
    those; the OLD fixture reports them | 0xffff0000 -> masked by run.js's
    FPU_STATUS_MASK the value becomes 0xFDD5 and can never match. Only the TAG word
    gets the kernel's convert_from_fxsr | 0xffff0000 -- the status word does not.

    the fdivp. The reference reports 0x7fff too; the NEW fixture's low half is
    0x7fff; the OLD fixture's low half is 0x0080, which is the raw ABRIDGED fxsave
    FTW byte, not the full tag word.
